# Retail Industry Talking Points

## Pain Points to Surface

Retail merchants at the mid-market level ($5M-$100M annual revenue) typically face these payment processing problems:

1. **Fragmented systems**: Separate processors for in-store POS, ecommerce, and mobile. Each has its own dashboard, reconciliation, and fee structure. Finance teams spend 10-15 hours per month reconciling across systems.

2. **Opaque pricing**: Most processors charge flat-rate fees that bundle interchange markup and hide the true cost. Merchants paying 2.9% flat on debit card transactions are overpaying by 1.0-1.5% compared to interchange-plus.

3. **Slow settlement**: T+2 or T+3 settlement is standard with legacy processors. For retailers managing tight inventory cycles and payroll, delayed access to funds creates cash flow strain.

4. **Limited fraud tools**: Basic AVS and CVV checks miss sophisticated fraud patterns. Retailers in card-not-present channels (ecommerce) see fraud rates of 1.5-2.5% without ML-based detection.

## Case Study: BrightMart (220-Store Regional Chain)

BrightMart migrated from a legacy processor (First Data / Fiserv) to NovaPay in March 2025.

Results after 6 months:
- Processing cost reduced by 22% ($38,000/month savings)
- Settlement moved from T+2 to T+1 (improved cash flow by ~$1.2M in float)
- Fraud losses dropped 71% after enabling ShieldAI
- Reconciliation time reduced from 12 hours/month to 2 hours/month (single dashboard)
- Integration completed in 3 weeks (POS terminals + ecommerce site)

Quote from BrightMart CFO: "We were paying Fiserv a hidden markup we didn't even know existed. NovaPay's interchange-plus model made our costs transparent overnight."

## Discovery Questions for Retail Prospects

Ask these during the meeting to understand the prospect's situation:
- How many locations do you operate? Are they all on the same POS system?
- What's your approximate monthly card processing volume?
- Do you sell online as well as in-store? What percentage of revenue is each?
- Who is your current payment processor? How long have you been with them?
- How do you currently handle chargebacks and fraud disputes?
- What does your settlement cycle look like? Is cash flow timing a concern?
- Are you planning any international expansion in the next 12-18 months?
