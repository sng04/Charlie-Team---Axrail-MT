# Competitor Comparison: NovaPay vs. Stripe vs. Square

| Feature | NovaPay | Stripe | Square |

| Online transaction fee | Interchange + 0.30% + $0.12 | 2.9% + $0.30 | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| In-person transaction fee | Interchange + 0.20% + $0.08 | 2.7% + $0.05 | 2.6% + $0.10 |

## Key Differentiators for NovaPay

### Where NovaPay Wins
- **Interchange-plus transparency**: Stripe and Square use flat-rate pricing which overcharges merchants on debit and low-interchange cards. NovaPay's interchange-plus model saves high-volume merchants 15-30% on processing costs.
- **Unified in-store + online**: Square is strong in-store, Stripe is strong online. NovaPay provides a single integration for both with cross-channel token recognition.
- **ShieldAI fraud engine**: Included in Growth and Enterprise plans. Stripe Radar is comparable but costs extra ($0.05/screened transaction on custom rules). Square's fraud tools are basic.
- **Enterprise-grade SLAs**: 99.99% uptime SLA on Enterprise plan. Stripe offers 99.99% but with less transparent incident communication.

### Where Competitors Win
- **International availability**: Stripe operates in 47 countries with multi-currency support. Square operates in 8 countries. NovaPay is US-only (Canada and UK planned Q3 2026).
- **Developer ecosystem**: Stripe has the largest developer community and most extensive documentation. NovaPay's docs are comprehensive but the community is smaller.
- **Hardware**: Square offers proprietary POS hardware (Square Terminal, Square Register). NovaPay is hardware-agnostic and partners with Verifone and Ingenico.
- **Banking services**: Square offers Square Banking (checking, savings, loans). Stripe offers Stripe Treasury. NovaPay does not offer banking services.

## Common Objections and Responses

**"Stripe is free to start, NovaPay Growth costs $149/month"**
Response: Stripe's flat 2.9% + $0.30 is more expensive per transaction for most merchants. A retailer processing $200K/month saves approximately $1,200/month with NovaPay's interchange-plus pricing — far more than the $149 platform fee.

**"We already use Stripe and switching is painful"**
Response: NovaPay provides a migration toolkit that maps Stripe customer tokens to NovaPay tokens. For Hosted Checkout integrations, the switch takes 2-3 days. For API integrations, our solutions team handles the migration at no cost for Enterprise plans.

**"What about international expansion? We plan to sell in Europe"**
Response: NovaPay's Canada and UK expansion is confirmed for Q3 2026. For immediate international needs, merchants can run NovaPay domestically and a second processor internationally, then consolidate to NovaPay as coverage expands.
