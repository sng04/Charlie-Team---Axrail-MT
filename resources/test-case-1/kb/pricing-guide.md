# NovaPay Pricing Guide

## Pricing Model

NovaPay uses interchange-plus pricing. This means merchants pay the card network's interchange fee plus a transparent NovaPay markup. There are no hidden fees, monthly minimums, or long-term contracts.

## Transaction Fees

| Channel | NovaPay Markup | Typical All-In Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Card-present (POS) | Interchange + 0.20% + $0.08 | ~2.10% + $0.08 |
| Card-not-present (Online) | Interchange + 0.30% + $0.12 | ~2.55% + $0.12 |
| ACH Direct Debit | 0.80% (capped at $5.00) | 0.80% (max $5) |
| Digital Wallet (Apple Pay, Google Pay) | Same as underlying card type | Same as card |

## Volume Discounts

Merchants processing over $1M/month receive automatic rate reductions:
- $1M–$5M/month: Markup reduced by 0.03%
- $5M–$20M/month: Markup reduced by 0.06%
- Over $20M/month: Custom negotiation required

## Chargeback Fees

- $15 per chargeback
- Chargeback fee waived if merchant wins the dispute
- Merchants with chargeback rates over 1.5% enter a monitoring program with additional $25/month fee

## No Hidden Fees

NovaPay does not charge: setup fees, annual fees, PCI compliance fees, statement fees, batch processing fees, or early termination fees. What you see in the transaction fee schedule is what you pay.
