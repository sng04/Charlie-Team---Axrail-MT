# GreenBuild Carbon Reporting Methodology

## Overview

GreenBuild provides carbon footprint measurement and ESG reporting services for manufacturing and industrial clients. Our methodology is aligned with the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard, the most widely used international accounting framework for greenhouse gas emissions.

## Emission Scopes

### Scope 1 — Direct Emissions
Emissions from sources owned or controlled by the company. In manufacturing, this primarily includes:
- On-site combustion of fossil fuels (natural gas boilers, diesel generators)
- Process emissions from chemical reactions (e.g., cement clinker production, steel reduction)
- Fugitive emissions from refrigerant leaks, methane from wastewater treatment
- Company-owned vehicle fleet

### Scope 2 — Indirect Energy Emissions
Emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling consumed by the company. We report both:
- **Location-based**: Using average grid emission factors for the facility's region
- **Market-based**: Using emission factors from contractual instruments (RECs, PPAs, green tariffs)

### Scope 3 — Value Chain Emissions
Emissions from sources not owned or controlled by the company but related to its activities. GreenBuild currently covers the following Scope 3 categories for manufacturing clients:
- Category 1: Purchased goods and services (supplier emissions)
- Category 4: Upstream transportation and distribution
- Category 5: Waste generated in operations
- Category 6: Business travel
- Category 7: Employee commuting

**Note:** Full Scope 3 reporting across all 15 categories is available as an advanced engagement. Categories 1 and 4 are the most material for manufacturing clients and are included in all standard engagements.

## Data Collection Process

1. **Facility inventory**: Identify all operational sites, production lines, and energy sources
2. **Activity data gathering**: Collect utility bills, fuel purchase records, production logs, travel records
3. **Emission factor application**: Apply region-specific and fuel-specific emission factors from EPA, IEA, or DEFRA databases
4. **Quality assurance**: Cross-check data against production output metrics (emissions intensity per unit produced)
5. **Reporting**: Generate GHG Protocol-compliant reports with year-over-year trend analysis

## Reporting Outputs

GreenBuild delivers the following for each engagement:
- Annual GHG Inventory Report (Scope 1, 2, and selected Scope 3)
- Executive Summary Dashboard (web-based, real-time updates)
- Regulatory Filing Packs (formatted for SEC, EPA, or EU CSRD as applicable)
- Reduction Roadmap with prioritized abatement measures and estimated ROI
