# GreenBuild Service Tiers & Pricing

## Standard Engagement
- **Target**: Single-facility or small multi-facility manufacturers (1-5 sites)
- **Scope**: Scope 1 + Scope 2 + Scope 3 Categories 1, 4, 5
- **Deliverables**: Annual GHG Inventory, Executive Dashboard, one regulatory filing pack
- **Timeline**: 10-13 weeks
- **Pricing**: $45,000 - $75,000 (depends on facility complexity)
- **Ongoing monitoring**: $2,500/month for dashboard updates and quarterly reviews

## Enterprise Engagement
- **Target**: Multi-facility manufacturers (6+ sites), supply chain-intensive operations
- **Scope**: Full Scope 1, 2, 3 (all 15 categories), scenario modeling
- **Deliverables**: All Standard outputs plus: full Scope 3 assessment, science-based target setting support, reduction roadmap with financial modeling, CSRD/ESRS gap analysis
- **Timeline**: 16-24 weeks
- **Pricing**: $120,000 - $250,000 (depends on site count and supply chain complexity)
- **Ongoing monitoring**: $5,000/month for continuous tracking and regulatory update service

## Add-On Services

| Service | Description | Price |
|---|---|---|
| CBAM Assessment | Calculate embedded emissions for EU-exported products | $15,000 per product line |
| SBTi Target Setting | Develop and submit Science Based Targets initiative commitment | $25,000 |
| Supply Chain Survey | Collect primary emission data from top 50 suppliers | $20,000 |
| Assurance Readiness | Prepare documentation for third-party limited assurance audit | $18,000 |
| Board Training | Half-day workshop on ESG governance for board of directors | $8,000 |

## Payment Terms

- 40% on project kickoff
- 30% at data collection completion
- 30% on final report delivery
- Ongoing monitoring billed quarterly in advance
