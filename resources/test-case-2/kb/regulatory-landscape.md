# Regulatory Landscape for Carbon Reporting

## United States

### SEC Climate Disclosure Rule (2024)
The SEC adopted rules requiring public companies to disclose climate-related risks and greenhouse gas emissions in annual filings. Key requirements:
- Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions (phased in by filer category)
- Large accelerated filers: Reporting begins fiscal year 2025
- Accelerated filers: Reporting begins fiscal year 2026
- Smaller reporting companies: Scope 1 and 2 only, begins fiscal year 2027
- Scope 3 emissions reporting was removed from the final rule after industry pushback
- Third-party assurance required for Scope 1 and 2 (limited assurance initially, reasonable assurance by 2033)

### EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program (GHGRP)
Facilities emitting 25,000 metric tons CO2e or more per year must report annually to EPA. This covers approximately 8,000 facilities representing 85% of US GHG emissions. Reporting is facility-level, not corporate-level.

### California Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act (SB 253)
Requires companies doing business in California with over $1 billion in annual revenue to report Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions starting in 2026 (Scope 3 starting 2027). This is more aggressive than the SEC rule because it includes Scope 3.

## European Union

### Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)
Replaces the Non-Financial Reporting Directive. Requires detailed ESG disclosures using the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS). Applies to:
- All large EU companies (>250 employees or >€40M revenue)
- Non-EU companies with >€150M EU revenue (starting 2028)
- Requires full Scope 1, 2, and 3 reporting with limited assurance

### EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)
Imposes a carbon price on imports of carbon-intensive goods (cement, steel, aluminum, fertilizers, electricity, hydrogen). Importers must purchase CBAM certificates corresponding to the carbon price that would have been paid if goods were produced under EU carbon pricing rules. Full implementation from January 2026.

## How GreenBuild Helps with Compliance

GreenBuild's reporting outputs are designed to satisfy multiple regulatory frameworks simultaneously:
- **SEC filers**: Annual GHG Inventory Report maps directly to SEC disclosure requirements
- **EPA GHGRP**: Facility-level data collection supports EPA reporting granularity
- **SB 253**: Full Scope 1-3 methodology covers California requirements
- **CSRD**: ESRS-aligned data points are captured during standard engagements (additional ESRS gap analysis available)
- **CBAM**: Embedded emissions calculations for exported goods can be generated on request

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

| Regulation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| SEC Climate Rule | Standard SEC enforcement (fines, restatements, consent orders) |
| EPA GHGRP | Up to $62,689/day per violation (adjusted annually for inflation) |
| SB 253 | Administrative penalties up to $500,000/year |
| CSRD | Member state-determined penalties (expected to mirror financial reporting violations) |
